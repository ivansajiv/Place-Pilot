# 🎯 PlacePilot | Placement Co-Pilot

**A smarter way to prepare for campus placements.**

PlacePilot is a student-focused placement preparation platform built to bring resume analysis, job matching, interview practice, and application tracking into one workspace. It combines practical placement tools with AI-powered career guidance to help students prepare with more clarity and confidence.

## ✨ Key Features

- **📄 Resume Analysis:** Upload PDF, DOCX, or TXT resumes and extract text to identify relevant technical skills.
- **🎯 Job Matching:** Explore role matches based on detected skills and tailor your preparation to selected opportunities.
- **📊 ATS Readiness:** View a resume readiness score and identify areas that may need improvement.
- **🎙️ Mock Interviews:** Practise technical and behavioural questions through structured interview drills, with a scorecard to review your responses.
- **🤖 AI Career Co-Pilot:** Get placement guidance, resume feedback, interview preparation tips, and coding strategy support through Gemini integration.
- **📅 Placement Calendar:** Organise placement drives, interview dates, and application deadlines in an interactive calendar.
- **📌 Application Tracking:** Keep track of active applications and monitor your placement preparation progress.
- **📝 Resume Builder:** Customise resumes using multiple templates and prepare PDF-ready layouts.
- **🌗 Personalised Interface:** Switch between Studio and Twilight themes with a responsive layout for different screen sizes.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** – Application structure
- **CSS3** – Responsive layouts, styling, and theme system
- **JavaScript** – Application logic and interactive features
- **Alpine.js** – Reactive UI and state management
- **Google Gemini API** – AI-powered career assistance
- **PDF.js** – PDF text extraction
- **Mammoth.js** – DOCX text extraction
- **Google Fonts** – Typography


## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal of PlacePilot is simple: make placement preparation more organised, accessible, and practical by bringing essential tools together in one place.

Instead of juggling separate tools for resumes, interview practice, and deadlines, students can use a single workspace to manage their preparation.
